# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Health-conscious shoppers checking whether a food, supplement, or cosmetic product is safe or worth buying — often while browsing online or standing in a store aisle. They want a fast, trustworthy verdict, not a lecture.

## Product Purpose

Gud For Us surfaces health scores, ingredient breakdowns, and personalized insights for 10M+ products worldwide. Success looks like a shopper leaving with confidence about what they're putting in or on their body.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, clean, warm — like a knowledgeable friend who happens to be a nutritionist. Science-backed but never cold.

## Anti-references

- Generic "health app" teal + white sterility (MyFitnessPal, old NHS pages)
- Aggressive red-flag fearmongering UIs
- Cluttered supplement-store aesthetics

## Design Principles

1. Verdict first — the score and outcome are the headline; everything else supports them.
2. Earn the data — show numbers only when they add meaning; never data for data's sake.
3. Warm precision — accuracy without clinical coldness; green and natural tones over hospital white.
4. Mobile trust — this is used at the shelf; hierarchy and tap targets must work at a glance.
5. No empty ink — every element on screen has a job; remove anything decorative that doesn't serve the verdict.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Color is never the sole indicator of meaning (always paired with label or icon). Support reduced-motion preference.
